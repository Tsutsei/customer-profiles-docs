This policy describes how the Atlassian Marketplace app "Customer Profiles for Jira Service Management" (the "App") processes data. The App is provided by **Bc. Tomáš Buchta, sole trader, Company ID (IČO) 21540951, registered office 30. dubna 2307/12, 702 00 Ostrava, Czech Republic** ("we"). Contact: **[support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net](mailto:support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net)**.

## 1. What the App stores
Only its **settings**, saved by Jira administrators: the names of the detail fields shown on requests and their order, highlighted field values (for example "Customer Segment = Enterprise"), account health thresholds and on/off switches. The App stores **no customer or organization data** and **no account IDs, names or e-mail addresses**.

## 2. What the App reads and changes
To show profiles, account health, the directory and exports, the App reads customers, organizations, their detail fields and requests (status, dates, SLAs, satisfaction ratings) **in the user's browser, with that user's permissions**. This data is shown to the user and, for exports, saved as a file by the user; it is never sent to or stored by the App's backend.

The import reads a file chosen by the user **in the browser** and, after the user confirms the preview, sets customer and organization detail field values in Jira with the user's permissions and, when the user chooses it, creates organizations, adds customers to organizations and service projects, and creates customer accounts (e-mail and name from the file). The file is not uploaded or stored. The change log is created in the browser and downloaded by the user.

## 3. Where the data is stored
Settings are stored in Atlassian's infrastructure (Forge Key-Value Store) and inherit Atlassian's data residency. The App does not send data to any server outside Atlassian, and we (the vendor) have no access to it.

## 4. Purpose and legal basis
Data is processed solely to provide the App's functions on behalf of the Jira site, which acts as the controller; we act as processor (Art. 28 GDPR) where personal data is involved (customer data displayed or changed in the browser, and any personal data an administrator types into a highlighted value).

## 5. Retention
Settings are kept until they are changed or the App is uninstalled. When the App is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the App's data according to its Forge data retention policy.

## 6. Data Processing Addendum
The [Data Processing Addendum]({{ '/dpa/' | relative_url }}) (Art. 28 GDPR) applies to all customers.

## 7. Sharing
We do not sell or share data. Sub-processor: Atlassian (hosting of the Forge platform).

## 8. Your rights
Customers' personal data stays in Jira Service Management and is managed there. For requests concerning personal data, contact your Jira site admin or us at [support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net](mailto:support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net).
